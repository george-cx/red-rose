We had the honour of tasting, together with my friend, a fifty-year-old fortified wine — Salkhino — seven times a gold medallist at international exhibitions, the pride of Baghdati and, to be more precise, of the village of Vartsikhe. It seems to be a habit of ours, somehow.

Yes, yes. You are not mistaken — a few bottles have stayed with me in the collection.

Aromas beyond words, flowering in the glass.
