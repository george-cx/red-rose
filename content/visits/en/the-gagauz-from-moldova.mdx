Gagauz from Moldova. Cheerful people, physically handsome — a Turkic-speaking nation mixed in among the Moldovans — and they joke with one another about as deftly as we do.

One said of the other: we grew up so close we wore one pair of pants between us.

Which, put our way, comes out as: we grew up in the same pair of pants.
