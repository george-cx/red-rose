## 10. The owner's voice

This matters as much as the facts, because I'll be writing in it.

- **Georgian, informal, spoken.** Commas without spaces, ellipses everywhere as breath marks
  rather than punctuation, sentences that trail off. He writes the way he talks.
- **Warm about people, unguarded about emotion.** "I was happy, happy from the heart, truly,
  truly" (P9). He admits to crying at applause (P26) and to being nervous when Italian wine
  professionals arrive (P32).
- **Funny, and often at his own expense.** The "bush, bush, bush" joke (P14), teaching a
  guest a rude Georgian phrase and then worrying about it (P7), the *mein lieber Führer*
  slip and the 12-year-old telling him off (P41).
- **Technically serious.** Post 3 is a genuine essay on mycorrhizal symbiosis. He does not
  simplify for the reader.
- **Proud without being a salesman.** He never once posts a price, a package or a call to
  book. The posts are notes about days that went well.
- **Openly religious and openly political.** He greets Orthodox feast days, mourns the
  Patriarch, and says plainly that the war in Ukraine is fratricidal and indefensible (P19).
- **Quotes the canon by heart** — Rustaveli, Ilia Chavchavadze, proverbs.
- **He does not market.** Nothing in 43 posts reads as promotion. Copy on this site should
  stay under that ceiling.

## 11. What the marani physically looks like

From the photographs — this is the only reliable guide for Phase 2, and it contradicts one
assumption in the brief.

- **Walls are dark volcanic rubble stone** — reddish-brown, plum, grey — in heavy irregular
  masonry, with brick infill. **Not lime-washed.** The brief anticipated white walls; the
  actual room is dark.
- **Ceilings are brick barrel vaults** and pale timber, low and warm.
- **Floors are pale mixed flagstone** — irregular slabs of cream, grey and rust, close to
  terrazzo. This is the **light** surface in the room, not the walls.
- **Qvevri mouths sit flush in the floor**, ringed with heavy stone collars; indoors they
  are ringed in **deep red**.
- **Light is warm tungsten**, low, pooled — spotlights and small chandeliers under the vault.
- **Wrought iron and glass** arched doors open to a green garden.
- **Tables are long, blond, raw timber**, packed edge to edge with food.
- **The house accent colour is deep red**: the qvevri collars, the aprons the children wear.
- Outdoors: vines, maize, tomatoes, a wide green valley, big skies.

**Design consequence.** The brief's instruction — "dark ground, one luminous colour" — holds,
but the palette should come off these photographs rather than off the generic marani: dark
plum-grey stone, warm lamp-light, pale stone floor, and the **red** that is already the
cellar's own. The wine in the glasses photographed is pink (the rosé) and dark garnet (the
Salkhino), not amber; the amber assumption in the brief is not supported by the source.
